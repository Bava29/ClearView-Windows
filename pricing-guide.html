UNDERSTANDING YOUR INVESTMENT

What Affects Window Pricing?

Every home is different. Understanding these key factors helps you choose the right windows for your needs and budget.

01

Window Type & Design

Casement, sliding, awning and picture windows have different designs, operating mechanisms and hardware requirements.

Style and functionality
02

Frame Material

Aluminium, uPVC, timber and composite frames differ in appearance, durability, maintenance needs and overall cost.

Material and finish
03

Glass & Energy Performance

Double or triple glazing, low-E coatings, acoustic glass and other performance features can influence the final price.

Comfort and efficiency
04

Size & Customisation

Larger windows, non-standard dimensions, special shapes and custom finishes may require additional materials or fabrication.

Dimensions and detailing
05

Installation Requirements

Removing old windows, repairing openings, site access and finishing work can affect the overall installation cost.

Labour and site conditions

Compare Window Options

Explore popular window styles and understand their practical benefits before making your investment.

Versatile Choice

Casement Windows

Hinged windows that open outward to provide ventilation and a clear outdoor view.

  • Excellent ventilation
  • Tight closure when properly installed
  • Multiple size options
Budget consideration Hardware & opening size
Space-Saving

Sliding Windows

Smooth horizontal operation makes these windows practical for rooms with limited exterior space.

  • Space-efficient operation
  • Simple everyday use
  • Suitable for wider openings
Budget consideration Track & panel configuration
Ventilation

Awning Windows

Top-hinged windows that open outward and can help provide ventilation during light rain, depending on conditions and design.

  • Useful for ventilation
  • Suitable for smaller openings
  • Can complement other window styles
Budget consideration Hinges & operating hardware
Maximum View

Picture Windows

Fixed windows designed to bring in natural light and create uninterrupted outdoor views.

  • Expansive outdoor views
  • No operating hardware
  • Multiple glazing choices
Budget consideration Glass size & specification

Actual costs vary by dimensions, frame material, glass specification, hardware and installation requirements. Request a personalised quote for accurate pricing.

What's Included in Your Window Quote?

A clear quotation helps you understand the full project cost, compare options and avoid unexpected expenses.

Window Products

Check the quantity, dimensions, selected window styles, frame materials and finishes.

Glass & Performance Features

Confirm glazing type, energy-efficiency options, hardware and any selected upgrades.

Installation & Labour

Ask whether fitting, labour, sealing and installation-related work are included.

Removal & Finishing

Verify charges for old window removal, disposal, opening repairs and finishing work.

Warranty & Payment Terms

Review warranty coverage, exclusions, payment schedule, taxes and project timelines.
