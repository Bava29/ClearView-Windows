THE CARE GUIDE

Small Details.
Lasting Care.

A little regular attention can help keep your windows looking good and operating smoothly. Make these simple checks part of your home maintenance routine.

PRACTICAL TIPS FOR EVERYDAY HOMEOWNERS
01

Clean Frames & Glass

Use a soft cloth, mild cleaning solution, and suitable methods recommended for your particular window material.

CLEANING
02

Check Moving Parts

Periodically check handles, hinges, locks, and tracks for dirt, looseness, or difficulty during normal operation.

OPERATION
03

Inspect Seals & Drainage

Look for visible seal damage and blocked drainage openings. Follow the manufacturer's maintenance guidance when cleaning them.

INSPECTION
04

Address Issues Early

If you notice cracks, persistent drafts, water ingress, or damaged hardware, arrange an appropriate inspection.

PREVENTION

Always follow the care instructions provided for your specific window type and finish.
